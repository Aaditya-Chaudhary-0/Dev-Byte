## Purpose
A mentor can offer free or paid mentoring sessions. A student can discover and book them.

## Offering
Title, description, topic/skills, duration, price, active/inactive.

Price 0 = free. Price > 0 = paid.

## Availability
Mentor defines recurring availability windows.

## Booking
Student selects offering + available slot.
Free: confirm without payment.
Paid: store payment state; real production payment/payout is outside the September 22 requirement. A sandbox/test flow may be used for the demo.

## Meeting
The current scope stores a Google Meet link. Automatic Google Calendar/Meet generation remains a future enhancement.

## Student
Find mentor, view offerings, book, upcoming/past, join Meet, feedback.

## Mentor
Create offering, availability, bookings, upcoming/past, basic earnings display if paid offerings are demonstrated.
